# Synthetic Causal Graph Notes

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the synthetic data-generation mechanism used by EconoCausal.
The graph is stored in synthetic_causal_graph.dot and generated by synthetic_causal_graph.py.

## 2. Variables

- Price: Product price generated by the synthetic data generator.
- Competitor Price: Calculated using price and a random multiplier.
- Promotion: Binary promotion indicator.
- Season: Categorical season: low, medium, or high.
- Discount: Treatment variable sampled independently.
- Purchase: Binary outcome sampled using the synthetic purchase probability.

## 3. Graph Relationships

- Price -> CompetitorPrice
- Price -> Purchase
- CompetitorPrice -> Purchase
- Promotion -> Purchase
- Season -> Purchase
- Discount -> Purchase

## 4. Treatment and Outcome

The treatment is discount. The outcome is purchase. Discount is sampled independently between 0 and 0.35. Purchase probability depends on discount, price, the competitor-price-to-price ratio, promotion, and season.

## 5. Interpretation Notes

This graph represents the synthetic data-generation process, not a verified real-world causal system.
The dataset supports testing the project data pipeline, model, and API. The graph alone does not prove real-world causality. Appropriate assumptions, reliable data, and further validation are required for a real-world causal interpretation.

## 6. Original Conceptual Graph

The original causal_graph.dot represents a conceptual business model where price, competitor price, promotion, and season may influence discount and purchase.
The synthetic graph differs because the current generator samples discount independently. Keeping both graphs separate prevents the synthetic mechanism from being confused with real-world pricing assumptions.

## 7. Conclusion

The synthetic causal graph documents the main dependencies in the current data generator while distinguishing synthetic data from real-world causal claims.
