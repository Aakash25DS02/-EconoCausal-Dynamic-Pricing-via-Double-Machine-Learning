# -EconoCausal-Dynamic-Pricing-via-Double-Machine-Learning
Dynamic Pricing via Double Machine Learning




Project Overview:
EconoCausal is an AI-powered causal inference platform designed to help businesses make ROI-driven marketing and customer retention decisions. It goes beyond traditional machine learning by identifying not just what might happen, but what changes because of a specific action. 

The Problem:
Traditional predictive ML models identify customers who are likely to leave or make a purchase, but they cannot determine whether a marketing offer will actually change their behavior. As a result, businesses may waste money offering discounts to customers who would stay anyway or fail to target customers who could be persuaded.

The Expected Outcome:
The platform identifies Persuadable customers whose behavior is likely to change because of an intervention. By targeting these customers with the most effective offers, EconoCausal helps businesses improve customer retention, maximize campaign ROI, and reduce unnecessary marketing expenses.

The Solution:
EconoCausal uses advanced Double Machine Learning (DML) algorithms to estimate the Individual Treatment Effect (ITE). It compares potential customer outcomes under different interventions, such as offering $10 versus $20 discounts, to estimate the actual impact of each offer on customer behavior.
