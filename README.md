# EconoCausal: Dynamic Pricing via Double Machine Learning

## The Problem
Traditional predictive ML models identify customers likely to purchase, but they do not necessarily determine whether a marketing offer will change customer behavior. Businesses may waste money discounting purchases that would happen anyway or miss customers who could be persuaded.

## The Solution
EconoCausal uses Double Machine Learning (DML) techniques to estimate the effect of discounts on customer purchase behavior. It compares outcomes under different discount scenarios to support data-driven pricing decisions.

## Important Note
The discount recommendation is based on model predictions and estimated revenue per record, not profit. It is not a guarantee of real-world results or proof of a causal effect.
